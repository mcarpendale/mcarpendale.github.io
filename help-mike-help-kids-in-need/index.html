The face of Chain Reaction Sydney 2023 challenge

We’re back at it….. raising funds for kids in need…….. And I NEED your HELP.

The Chain Reaction Challenge for 2023 has officially launched — and get this — Chain Reaction (reasons still unknown) has selected moi as the face, or 🤔 perhaps the beard of their Sydney event……. Not wanting to be rude, I’m backing up my inaugural 2022 ride and joining the team again for 2023. Ride on!!

Chain Reaction 2022 lured me outside for an amazing, extremely safe and well run event.

With the help of Pure Storage, Pure Good and Chain Reaction, I’m going to tackle this challenge again while raising funds for kids in need.

So after that horrible experience in 2020 of a cycling hit and run, where I became a zwift recluse — I’ll say “see ya later” to both the indoor trainer and Queensland weather again. I’m joining the Sydney crew and will be tackling the beautiful (😏 aka harsh, rugged, chilly) Aotearoa on a 1,000+km course with 14,000+m of climbing, all in only 7 days.

Funds raised from this event will support major charity partners, So They Can and **BaptistCare HOPESTREET. **It’s with YOUR help we can make a real difference to the lives of these children and the charities that support them.

Every little bit helps and I would be so grateful for your support, this link 👇 will take you to my fundraising page

https://www.chain-reaction.org.au/fundraisers/mikecarpendale/2023-sydney

Thanks, Mike
