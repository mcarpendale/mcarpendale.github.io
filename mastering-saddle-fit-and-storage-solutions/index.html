Mastering Saddle Fit and Storage Solutions

In the world of high-performance cycling and cutting-edge technology, there’s one common thread that binds leaders like Specialized and Pure Storage;

A relentless pursuit of customer satisfaction, underpinned by the confidence of a “Right-Size Guarantee”.

Though Specialized might not use this exact term, their approach screams it loud and clear. As a tech blogger for Pure Storage with a love for cycling, I’ve lived the reality of these guarantees both on the saddle and behind the screen.

Why, you might ask, am I drawing parallels between my day job at Pure Storage and weekend escapades on two wheels? Well, it all started with my new(ish) bike and its stock saddle, a combo that, let’s just say, wasn’t doing my sitbones any favours.

the new(ish) additionCue the eye-rolling from my turn to cycling advisor — Chriso, who’s been advising me for years about getting a professional bike fit. Ignoring sage advice, I persevered, but after a year of discomfort, I decided it was time for a change.

The last thing I wanted was to be that guy on the Facebook marketplace, offloading a “barely sat on” saddle. So, the quest began. After a few false starts with shops more interested in quick sales than actual solutions, I found my way to PedalHeads. Shuan, the guru of comfort, introduced me to the Retül fitment program by Specialized and their almost too-good-to-be-true saddle exchange policy. The deal? Get measured, pick a saddle, and if it doesn’t feel like sitting on a cloud, exchange it until you find “the one”. If bliss remains elusive, you get your money back.

Shuan, PedalHeads and Specialized, armed with their Retül program, transformed what I initially dreaded as a PITA quest (literally and figuratively) into a pain-free experience — again, both literally and figuratively. Their customer-centric approach, ensuring that every rider finds their perfect saddle fit, mirrored the ethos we uphold at Pure Storage. It’s not just about making a sale; it’s about making a customer happy for the long haul.

This got me thinking about the similarities — and how we at Pure Storage uniquely address the challenges of data storage. Our commitment is embodied in our RIGHT-SIZE GUARANTEE (RSG), ensuring clients receive precisely what they need without the risk of over-provisioning or underutilisation. It’s this pursuit of the perfect balance that sets us apart.

The essence of our RSG is to uncomplicate data storage, aligning perfectly with our mission. This guarantee isn’t just about meeting immediate needs; it’s about anticipating and adapting to our customers’ evolving storage requirements with a process that’s straightforward and user-friendly.

The flexibility of the RSG is a testament to our customer-centric approach, offering a 12-month window from shipment for clients to fully experience and benefit from our data reduction technology. This period allows for a seamless migration and adaptation process, showcasing the tangible benefits of our storage solutions.

Moreover, our RSG is complemented by the LOVE YOUR STORAGE GUARANTEE (LYSG) — for the first product purchased, it allows for a 30-day ‘money-back’ option. This bold promise goes beyond conventional satisfaction guarantees, reflecting the confidence we have in our products.

For our customers, the advantages of embracing the RSG and LYSG are transformative. By eliminating the need for time-consuming proof of concepts, we fast-track the journey to realisation. This addresses any potential doubts about data reduction and capabilities, ultimately simplifying the path to uncomplicate data storage forever.

Both in the saddle and in storage, the RIGHT-SIZE GUARANTEE is more than a policy; it’s a promise — a commitment to excellence and customer satisfaction.

If you want to get in touch to see what Pure Storage can do for you, I’m merely a connection request and DM away.

So, whether you’re a cyclist or a tech enthusiast, remember this: the right support makes any journey more enjoyable…. knowing you’ve got a partner who’s got your back makes all the difference.

Here’s to finding your perfect match, on the road and in the DC.
